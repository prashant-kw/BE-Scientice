Welcome to the Global Cardiology Bulletin. In today's report, we review the critical evidence shaping heart failure care, prevention strategies, and digital cardiology. Earlier cardiovascular risk assessment remains a major clinical priority, enabling proactive intervention before structural disease advances. At the same time, guideline-directed heart failure therapy continues to demonstrate substantial improvements in long-term patient outcomes. Advancements in remote monitoring are rapidly expanding patient access to specialist cardiac care, bridging critical diagnostic gaps. Ultimately, all clinical decisions should remain closely tailored to the individual patient and current medical guidelines. Thank you for joining us.
