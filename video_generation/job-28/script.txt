Effect of Different Treatment Durations of Sodium–Glucose Cotransporter 2 Inhibitor on Cardiovascular Outcomes in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes: A Target Trial Emulation Study
B. Hong, K. Jung, H. Lee, J. H. Bae, Y. M. Cho
Longer treatment duration with sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) was associated with progressively lower risks of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and hospitalization for heart failure (HHF) among people with type 2 diabetes, according to a nationwide analysis from South Korea.
